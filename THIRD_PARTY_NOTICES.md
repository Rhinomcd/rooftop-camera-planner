# Third-party notices

## Rooftop Camera Planner

Rooftop Lens is a fork and rewrite of Rooftop Camera Planner by MVP IT Solutions
(https://github.com/MichaelRVP/rooftop-camera-planner, version 0.1.3). The idea of
learning a low-travel camera view from complete laps and marking the click route comes
from that project. Its course table, with its ordered obstacle steps and Falador's
alternate ledges, is carried over in `Course.java`. Its copyright notice is kept in
`LICENSE` and in the header of `Course.java`.
